Tired of full bags and digging for food?

Rucksack takes care of the chores every adventurer repeats all day. Made for World of Warcraft Forever.

Get it on CurseForgeDownload the zip

What it does

Bags

One bag window with your items in groups, your own groups too, a Hidden bag, and your bank and other characters a click away.

Restock

Say how much water, food or arrows to carry. A vendor that sells it tops you up, and it never spends below the gold you keep.

Clean-up

Keeps a few bag slots free. Junk is sold at the next vendor or the cheapest goes first; quest items stay.

Rations

Picks the right food and water and eats and drinks with one click.

Darkmoon Faire

Your tickets, where to get every turn-in item, and the prize you are saving for.

Scrolls

Mages decipher their scrolls with one click.

Every part is a module: tick the ones you want in Settings, and the rest are not loaded at all. Open Rucksack with /ruck.
